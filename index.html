FAQ

FAQ

Frequently asked questions about wholesale, product quality, and custom services.

What is the MOQ?
The minimum order quantity is 100g per color for stock items. You may mix multiple colors in a single order.
How long is production?
Production time varies by product, typically 3–7 days. Worldwide shipping via DHL/FedEx takes 3–5 business days.
Do you support OEM/private label?
Absolutely. We provide custom packaging, branding, and labeling for your salon or distribution business. The MOQ for private label is 100g per SKU. Our simple 4-step process: (1) Send your logo and packaging requirements; (2) We create pre-production samples for your approval (3–5 days); (3) After your confirmation, we begin full production; (4) Final QC photos are sent before shipping. We handle everything from design files to barcode generation.
Is the hair 100% human hair?
Yes. All our hair is 100% human hair with intact cuticle alignment. No silicone coating and no synthetic fibers.
How do you ensure consistent quality?
We follow a strict 7-step QC process: raw material inspection → sorting → structure check → wash verification → AQL 2.5 sampling → final touch test → packaging integrity check. Our defect rate stays below 1.5%. If you are not satisfied, we will rework or replace free of charge.
Can you do custom colors?
Yes. Send photos or physical samples and our colorists will match the shade. Custom colors typically add 2–3 business days to production.
